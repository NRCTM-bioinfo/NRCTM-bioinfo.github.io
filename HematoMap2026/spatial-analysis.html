Chapter 4 Spatial analysis

This chapter covers spatial transcriptomic data preprocessing, clustering, visualization, and cell-type signature scoring.

4.1 Preprocessing

Visium HD outputs are imported into Seurat, and spatial coordinates and tissue images are prepared for quality assessment and visualization. The workflow includes expression normalization, variable gene selection, dimensionality reduction, clustering, and visualization of clusters and selected gene expression patterns in tissue sections.
